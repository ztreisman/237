## boundary_racing.html — interactive visualization

Built a standalone HTML animation of the ring-4 and ring-5 boundary cycles
as a racing game. All geometry is inlined — no server needed, open in any
browser.

### Geometry
- Ring-4 track: 147 vertices, arc length 36.7 (normalized to unit sphere)
- Ring-5 track: 385 vertices, arc length 69.7
- Both tracks centered and scaled to unit sphere from ring-0 vertex
- Track data pre-computed from physics_grow_seed02_ring04_std0.0031.ply
  and physics_grow_seed03_ring05_std0.0415.ply

### Features
- Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom (3D projection, painter's algorithm)
- Glowing dots with fading trails race along both boundary cycles
- Finish line: checkerboard flag on ring-5 track, adjustable position
- Flash announcement on lap completion
- Pause/play, reset view, trails toggle
- Surface fill toggle: R4 surface and R5 surface independently,
  viridis coloring by ring depth, 42-55% opacity, painter's sort
- Speed slider: logarithmic, center = 1x = 60-second laps, range ÷10 to ×10
- Ring-4 and ring-5 racer count sliders (1–20 each)
- Ring-4 and ring-5 palette selectors: Rainbow, Viridis, Stoplight,
  Tron, Zissou, Grand Budapest, Moonrise, Darjeeling
  Default: Grand Budapest (ring 4), Tron (ring 5)
- Each racer gets a different color offset within the palette
- Three leaderboards (monospaced tabular format):
    Overall | Ring 4 | Ring 5
  Each shows top 4: rank | swatch | name | laps | best lap time
  Gold/silver/bronze medals for top 3. Best lap measured from
  second crossing onward. Reset laps button clears all.

### Mathematical content
The game makes the spherical gap argument visually immediate:
- Ring-4 track is a compact loop, racers complete laps quickly
- Ring-5 track winds all over the sphere, 2.6× longer arc
- The finish line represents the 12.35° spherical gap — the only
  uncovered region after ring-5 fills the sphere
- Toby's question "where is the finish line?" captures the geometry:
  the gap is where the ring-5 track almost meets itself but doesn't
- π₀ of the ring-5 boundary = {pt}: single connected component,
  one closed loop (unknot bounding an embedded disk)
- Surface fill shows the crumpled geometry directly: ring-4 is
  relatively coherent, ring-5 is extremely buckled
